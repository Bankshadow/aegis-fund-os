# ICP

Qualified:

- Thai grid-trading **students** who want to see why a grid fails under
  real costs, walk-forward, and a declared gate
- Educators who need evidence-backed lessons, not a bot that "just works"
- Operators researching fund-ops / audit / paper-grid plumbing

Bad-fit:

- Anyone who wants the agent to send live orders or handle other people's money
- Anyone shopping for a promoted dual/RL config to paste onto another
  scale or timeframe
- Anyone who wants unused dashboard chrome, UGC ad factories, or a
  10-bot GTM swarm attached to this repo

A won week: a student can state the two measured claims (fees are not the
cause; in-sample geometry pick is chance) and point at the fold table.
Not a won week: a new page nobody asked for, or a specialist bot that
posts, spends, or trades.
